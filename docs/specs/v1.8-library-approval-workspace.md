# v1.8.0 — Library as an Approval Workspace

Tracking issue: [#139](https://github.com/bradreimer/immich-dog-tagger/issues/139).

## Purpose

The Library today is a flat, photo-first grid: every classified crop in one paginated list,
corrected one card at a time through a per-card dropdown (`ui/src/features/library/LibraryPage.tsx`,
`components/LibraryEntryCard.tsx`). It browses well and works badly — labeling cost scales with the
number of **photos**, not the number of **pets**, and the maximum confirmation rate anywhere in the
app is one photo per interaction.

Every comparable faces workflow (Lightroom Classic, Immich, Apple Photos) works the other way
round: pick a subject, then confirm a **group** of proposed photos in one action. This spec turns
the Library into an identity-first approval workspace — **select a species → select a dog or cat →
approve clusters of recommendations for that pet** — sortable by capture date or confidence.

Clustering runs over `CropClassification.embedding`, which already exists for classified crops: no
new model, no new pipeline stage, no change to classification policy. A cluster approval routes
through the existing `ClassificationCorrectionService`, so it is N ordinary corrections with
ordinary provenance, not a new kind of state.

## User story

As the owner of a large library,
I want to pick one of my pets and confirm a whole group of proposed photos in a single action,
so that the work I do scales with how many pets I have rather than with how many photos they
appear in.

## Goals

- Make the **subject** the primary axis of the Library: species and pet are a selection step, not
  two filters among four.
- Let one human decision settle many photos, by proposing clusters of visually similar
  recommendations for the selected pet and approving a cluster in one action.
- Keep every approval an ordinary correction: same service, same provenance, same learning
  examples. Clustering proposes **groupings only** — it never writes a prediction, never
  manufactures a confidence value, and never assigns an identity without a human action.
- Close the supporting gaps the same analysis found: no negative signal (rejection), no identity
  merge, no rescue for a photo the detector missed, and coverage that is never measured.

## Non-goals

- Removing or changing the review queue. `/review` stays exactly as it is.
- Changing classification policy, thresholds, or the embedding model as part of the core workflow
  change.
- Changing `GET /api/library`'s query semantics.
- A fully automatic, Apple-style model that labels without the owner. Operator controls, jobs, and
  diagnostics are a differentiator for a self-hosting owner, not debt.

## Requirements

Each requirement is tracked as its own issue. Core workflow, in dependency order:

- **FR-1 — Library scoped to one pet** ([#140](https://github.com/bradreimer/immich-dog-tagger/issues/140)).
  The Library opens on a species (Dog / Cat / All) and pet selection, and everything it shows is
  scoped to that selection. Detailed below.
- **FR-2 / FR-3 — Cluster and approve** ([#141](https://github.com/bradreimer/immich-dog-tagger/issues/141)).
  Cluster the recommendations for the selected pet over the existing crop embeddings, and approve a
  whole cluster in one action via `ClassificationCorrectionService`.
- **FR-4 — Refine a cluster before approving** ([#142](https://github.com/bradreimer/immich-dog-tagger/issues/142)).
  Select and deselect individual photos within a proposed cluster.
- **FR-5 — Sorting** ([#143](https://github.com/bradreimer/immich-dog-tagger/issues/143)).
  Sort clusters and photos by capture date (oldest→newest, newest→oldest) or by confidence.
- **FR-6 — Rejection** ([#144](https://github.com/bradreimer/immich-dog-tagger/issues/144)).
  Reject a recommendation as "not this pet" — the negative signal the workflow has never had.
- **FR-7 — Cold start** ([#145](https://github.com/bradreimer/immich-dog-tagger/issues/145)).
  Cluster unlabeled crops for a pet with no examples yet, the case where the owner's cost is
  highest.
- **FR-8 — Honest coverage** ([#146](https://github.com/bradreimer/immich-dog-tagger/issues/146)).
  Report detection coverage, not just automation rate over already-detected crops.
- **FR-9 — Sensitivity and follow-up reclassification** ([#149](https://github.com/bradreimer/immich-dog-tagger/issues/149)).
  Owner-facing tagging sensitivity, and reclassify automatically after a review batch.
- **FR-10 — Merge identities** ([#148](https://github.com/bradreimer/immich-dog-tagger/issues/148)).
- **FR-11 — Rescue a missed detection** ([#147](https://github.com/bradreimer/immich-dog-tagger/issues/147)).
  Tag a photo whose pet the detector missed.

### FR-1 in detail

FR-1 is the information-architecture half of the feature: it ships no clustering and no bulk
action, it builds the surface the rest land on.

1. The Library presents a species chooser (Dog / Cat / All) and, within the chosen species, a pet
   chooser listing the active identities from `getDogs({ includeInactive: false })`.
2. Selecting a pet scopes the photos shown to that pet and that species, via the `identity` and
   `species` parameters `GET /api/library` already accepts. No new endpoint.
3. The flat "all photos" view remains reachable as an explicit choice alongside the pets, and its
   review-status filter, capture-date filters, and pagination keep working exactly as they do
   today.
4. Changing species clears a pet selection that no longer applies, rather than leaving a dog
   selected under Cats.
5. Changing any selection resets pagination — the existing offset-reset behavior must not regress.
6. A library with no identities configured shows a clear empty state pointing at Dogs & Cats, not
   a blank chooser.
7. The selection is workspace state that the later requirements read from, not state local to a
   filter row.

## Acceptance criteria

- **FR-1**: the Library opens on a species and pet selection; selecting a pet scopes the grid;
  the flat all-photos view, its filters, and pagination still work; changing species clears a
  now-invalid pet; every selection change resets pagination; an identity-less library points the
  owner at Dogs & Cats. Vitest covers the species/identity invalidation, the pagination reset, and
  the empty-identities state.
- **FR-2/FR-3**: approving a cluster of N recommendations produces N corrections through
  `ClassificationCorrectionService` with normal provenance, and no classification is written
  without a human action.
- Every requirement leaves `/review` and `GET /api/library`'s query semantics unchanged.

## Open questions

- Which clustering algorithm, and whether it runs on demand or as a job with cached assignments.
  Settle before #141 starts.
- Whether a rejection (FR-6) belongs in `ReviewAction` as a new action type or in its own table.
  It changes what "reviewed" means for every count derived from `ReviewAction` — the class of
  subtle breakage DT-1115 and #116 already hit. Settle before #142 or #144 merges.
