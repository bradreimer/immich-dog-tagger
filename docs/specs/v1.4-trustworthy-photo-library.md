# v1.4 Trustworthy Photo Library

> **Superseded in part by [v1.5-automatic-temporal-classification.md](v1.5-automatic-temporal-classification.md).**
> FR-4/FR-5 below and the Non-goals bullet about active ranges being "explicit owner input --
> never inferred" described DT-1114's manual `active_from`/`active_until` mechanism. v1.5 removes
> that mechanism and replaces it with automatic per-example temporal-recency weighting. The rest
> of this spec (FR-1 through FR-3, the library, corrections) is unaffected.

## Purpose

A mock user interview surfaced a mismatch between how Dog Tagger is built and how its owner
actually thinks about it. The app is built around a review *queue*: a stream of pending items to
triage and clear. The owner's mental model is different -- they want to **build and maintain a
trustworthy, searchable library of dog- (and cat-) tagged photos**, correcting mistakes whenever
they notice them (often long after the original review, e.g. when they spot a photo in the wrong
Immich album), and using the photo's capture date as a first-class piece of evidence when two
individuals look alike. This spec captures that shift and the concrete requirements it implies.

This is a UX/product-model shift, not a rewrite: the underlying pipeline (detect -> classify ->
review -> reclassify -> sync) and its data model are sound and unchanged. What changes is which
surface is primary (a searchable library, not a queue), what evidence is shown during correction
(capture date, prominently), and when corrections are allowed (any time, not just once).

## User story

As the owner, I want to browse and search everything Dog Tagger has ever classified -- not just
what's still pending -- see each photo's capture date next to its predicted identity, and fix a
wrong tag whenever I notice one (including photos I already reviewed weeks ago), so that I end up
with a library I trust rather than a queue I've merely cleared.

## Goals

- Show each photo's capture date prominently everywhere a prediction or identity is shown, as a
  primary piece of evidence for telling visually similar individuals apart (not buried in an
  export/metadata view).
- Provide a searchable, filterable library of every classified photo -- reviewed and unreviewed,
  confident and uncertain -- as a durable browsing surface, not a transient queue that empties out.
- Let the owner correct any classification's identity at any time, including ones already
  reviewed, from the library itself -- and have that correction reliably fix Immich album
  membership on the next sync, not just add a new album while leaving the photo in the old one.
- Use a dog's or cat's known active date range (when photo evidence establishes one) as an
  additional classification signal: down-rank or flag a candidate identity whose active range
  can't include the photo's capture date, instead of silently proposing a temporally impossible
  match.
- Preserve the existing review queue's value as a *triage* tool (surfacing what most needs
  attention) by keeping it available as a filtered view, while no longer treating it as the
  product's primary or only surface.

## Non-goals

- **The review queue is not removed.** It remains valuable for triage ("what most needs my
  attention right now") and keeps its existing keyboard-driven correction flow
  ([review-workflow.md](review-workflow.md)). What changes is that it stops being the *only* way
  to see or act on classified photos -- the library, covered by this spec, becomes the primary,
  persistent surface; the queue becomes one filtered view within that broader mental model.
- **No undo/redo for classification actions.** Explicitly ruled out during the interview.
  Correcting a wrong tag (this spec's library-editing requirement) already covers the real need
  (fix a mistake whenever it's noticed) without a general action-history/undo system. A correction
  is itself just another correction, using the exact mechanism the app already has.
- **No automatic date-of-birth/date-of-death inference or computer-vision age estimation.** An
  identity's active date range (see Requirements) is derived only from photo evidence already in
  the system (reviewed examples) or explicit owner input -- never inferred from the image content
  itself. Estimating a dog's age from a photo is a different, much harder problem and out of scope.
- **No change to the underlying detect/classify/reclassify/sync pipeline mechanics** beyond adding
  a capture-date signal to classification (see Requirements) and fixing stale Immich album
  membership on re-sync after a correction (see DT-1113). The nearest-neighbor embedding approach,
  confidence threshold, and Reclassify's idempotency guarantees are unchanged.
- **No full-text/OCR search over photo content.** "Searchable" means filterable by identity,
  species, date range, and review status -- not visual or textual content search.
- **No general-purpose species config change.** Date-range modeling applies per identity
  regardless of species, using the same hardcoded `dog`/`cat` species from DT-1110 -- this spec
  does not revisit that decision.

## Requirements

### FR-1: Photo capture date shown wherever a prediction is shown

Every place the UI shows a classification's predicted (or corrected) identity -- the Review card,
the library (FR-2), and the export view (`review_export.py`) -- must also show that photo's
capture date, sourced from `Asset.captured_at` (already populated from Immich's `fileCreatedAt` by
`scanner.py`; no new data source). When `captured_at` is null (Immich asset has no capture-date
metadata), the UI shows an explicit "date unknown" state rather than a blank field or a
misleading fallback (e.g. file-download time).

### FR-2: Searchable, paginated library of all classified photos

A new library surface lists every crop that has been classified (`CropClassification`), not just
pending ones, with:

- Filters: identity (exact match, matching the existing `ReviewQueryService.classifications()`
  `identity` filter), species, review status (reviewed / not yet reviewed, derived from
  `ReviewAction` existence exactly as `ReviewQueryService._has_review_action()` does today), and a
  capture-date range.
- Pagination (offset/limit) -- the existing `classifications()`/`active_review()` methods only
  support `limit`, with no `offset`, because a bounded queue never needed one; a library covering
  the full classified history does.
- Each result shows the same evidence a review item shows today (photo, species, predicted
  identity, confidence, capture date per FR-1) plus whether it's been reviewed and by what action.

### FR-3: Edit any previously assigned tag from the library

Every library item (reviewed or not) exposes the same identity-correction control the Review page
uses today, calling the existing `POST /classifications/{id}/correct` endpoint --
`ClassificationCorrectionService.correct()` already supports re-correcting an already-reviewed
classification with no changes needed (it records a new `ReviewAction`, updates
`CropClassification.identity`, and `Learner.learn_image()`'s `_forget_other_identities()` already
removes the stale reference example for the old identity). The gap this spec closes is downstream:
`SyncService`/`AlbumService` currently only ever *add* an asset to its newly-computed album and
never remove it from a stale one, so correcting "Fibs" to "Hermann" on an already-synced photo
leaves it in both the `Dog - Fibs` and `Dog - Hermann` Immich albums -- exactly the "photo in the
wrong album" complaint from the interview. Fixing this is part of DT-1113, not optional polish.

### FR-4: Capture date as a classification signal

`IdentityClassifier.classify()` gains an optional `captured_at` parameter for the crop being
classified. When an identity has a known active date range (FR-5) and the crop's `captured_at`
falls outside it, that candidate is flagged rather than silently ranked as usual -- surfaced in the
review/library reason (alongside today's `unknown` / `low-confidence` / `candidate-conflict`
reasons from `ReviewQueryService._review_reason()`) so the owner sees *why* a match looks
questionable instead of just a raw similarity score. A missing `captured_at` (no EXIF/Immich date)
or a candidate identity with no active range set never excludes or penalizes a candidate --
absence of date evidence must fail open, not closed, to avoid regressing accuracy for the (likely
common) case of photos or identities without date metadata.

### FR-5: Owner-editable active date range per identity

Add an optional active-date range (`active_from`, `active_until`, both nullable) to `Identity`,
editable from the existing Dogs & Cats management page (`DogManagementCard.tsx`), following the
same additive-migration pattern DT-1110 used for `Identity.species`. Both ends are optional and
independently settable (e.g. "started appearing in the library around 2019, still active" needs
only `active_from`). This is explicit owner input, not inferred (see Non-goals).

## Acceptance criteria

- A photo's capture date is visible on the Review card, in every library result, and in the
  review export, with an explicit "date unknown" state when `Asset.captured_at` is null.
- The library lists both reviewed and unreviewed classified photos, supports filtering by
  identity/species/review-status/date-range, and paginates correctly across a result set larger
  than one page.
- Correcting an identity from the library works identically to correcting from the Review page
  (same endpoint, same learning-example update), on both already-reviewed and not-yet-reviewed
  items.
- Re-running sync after a library correction removes the asset from its previous identity's
  Immich album and adds it to the new one -- verified by a test, not just by the add-side behavior
  as today.
- A candidate identity with a set active date range that cannot include the photo's capture date
  is flagged in the review/library reason, not silently presented as a normal high-confidence
  match; a candidate or photo missing date data is scored exactly as before this spec (no
  regression from the date signal being unavailable).
- The review queue continues to work exactly as it does today (`GET /review`, correct, skip,
  keyboard shortcuts) -- it becomes one entry point among several, not a removed feature.

## Open questions

- Should the library become the app's default landing page (replacing Overview), or a new sidebar
  entry alongside the existing five? This spec assumes the latter (additive nav entry) since the
  interview didn't ask to remove Overview's operational-dashboard role, but it's worth confirming
  before FR-2's frontend work starts.
- Should a date-range conflict ever *exclude* a candidate outright (not just flag it), once the
  owner has demonstrated enough trust in the signal? Starting flag-only (per FR-4) is the safer
  default; hard-excluding is a plausible follow-up once there's real usage data on false-positive
  rate.
- Should `active_from`/`active_until` ever be suggested automatically from the *earliest and
  latest* `captured_at` among an identity's existing reviewed examples (a passive hint the owner
  can accept, not an inference from image content)? Explicitly out of scope for the first cut
  (Non-goals), but the data already exists to compute it later.
- Item 5 from the interview ("reliably distinguish individual dogs... automatically organize into
  the appropriate Immich albums") is this spec's overarching goal, not a separately scoped ticket
  -- FR-3/DT-1113 fixes the concrete album-correctness gap found while researching this spec, and
  FR-4/DT-1114 is the concrete accuracy lever the interview proposed. If the owner wants dedicated
  investment in raw classification accuracy beyond that (e.g. embedding model changes, threshold
  retuning, per-identity precision/recall reporting), that already has a home in
  [reference-example-management.md](reference-example-management.md) and
  [active-learning-workflow-refinements.md](active-learning-workflow-refinements.md) and should be
  scoped there once a concrete lever is identified, rather than as a vague ticket here.
